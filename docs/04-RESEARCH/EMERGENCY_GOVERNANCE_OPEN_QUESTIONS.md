# Emergency Governance Open Questions

This register deliberately preserves unresolved questions. It does not authorize emergency action.

## Activation

- What objective conditions distinguish an unprecedented emergency from an ordinary high-priority event?
- Who can declare that the normal F0–F9 path is temporarily impracticable?
- What minimum evidence is required when time is severely constrained?

## Authority

- Which authority may activate the exception?
- How does delegated authority work if the normal leadership chain is unavailable?
- What prevents a senior individual from declaring an emergency merely to bypass ordinary governance?

## Time bounds

- What is the maximum initial duration?
- When must an emergency decision automatically expire?
- What evidence is required for renewal?

## Operational controls

- What actions are prohibited even during an emergency?
- What minimum logging must occur before, during, and after the action?
- What monitoring and stop conditions are mandatory?
- How should rollback work when rollback is technically impossible?

## Post-event learning

- Who conducts the independent review?
- How are emergency decisions evaluated without hindsight bias?
- When does an emergency decision become a new F0 observation?
- What threshold is required before an emergency lesson can enter F0–F9?

## Abuse resistance

- How do we detect repeated or fabricated emergency declarations?
- How are conflicts of interest handled?
- What happens when an emergency authority abuses temporary powers?
- How is institutional memory protected if the event reveals a governance failure?

## Success condition for future implementation

The future project should demonstrate that BHG can act under extreme uncertainty without creating a permanent bypass around evidence-based governance.
