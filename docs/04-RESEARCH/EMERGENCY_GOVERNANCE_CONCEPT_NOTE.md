# Emergency Governance Concept Note

## Purpose

Record, without adopting, the concept of a governed emergency path for genuinely unprecedented events in which normal F0–F9 evidence-gated progression cannot provide a timely response.

## Problem statement

The normal F0–F9 lifecycle is intentionally evidence-driven. A sufficiently novel crisis may have no historical precedent, insufficient data, or insufficient time to perform a controlled F4 experiment. A governance system that requires prior precedent in every circumstance could become a source of operational paralysis.

## Candidate concept

Create a parallel **Emergency Governance / Exceptional Decision Protocol** that permits bounded, temporary action when the risk of inaction materially exceeds the risk of acting under uncertainty.

The concept does **not** grant unrestricted authority to leadership. It proposes that exceptional action be:

- explicitly triggered;
- justified against available evidence and known unknowns;
- attributed to an authorized decision-maker;
- time-bounded;
- reversible where practicable;
- continuously logged;
- monitored against predefined stop conditions;
- reviewed after the event;
- converted into institutional learning when appropriate.

## Relationship to F0–F9

This concept is a parallel path, not F10 and not a replacement for F0–F9.

```text
NORMAL:      F0 → F1 → F2 → F3 → F4 → F5 → F6 → F7 → F8 → F9
EXCEPTION:   EVENT → EMERGENCY GATE → TEMPORARY ACTION → REVIEW → F0
```

A successful emergency response may generate a new observation or evidence base that enters the normal lifecycle later.

## Human judgment

Human intuition is not treated as institutional evidence. It may function as a hypothesis, warning signal, or emergency decision input when precedent is absent. Permanent policy still requires evidence, governance authority, and traceability.

## Required future safeguards

Any future implementation should investigate at minimum:

1. activation criteria;
2. emergency authority and succession;
3. maximum decision duration;
4. automatic sunset;
5. renewal conditions;
6. documentation minimums under time pressure;
7. monitoring and stop conditions;
8. rollback requirements;
9. conflicts of interest;
10. post-event independent review;
11. conversion of lessons into F0–F9;
12. protection against abuse of emergency powers.

## Current decision

This document records an architectural idea only. It is **not** a standard, policy, authority delegation, operational procedure, or permission to bypass BHG Governance.

`operational_use: PROHIBITED`

## Future project trigger

Implementation should not begin merely because this concept exists. A future project should first define and test the emergency model, its authority boundaries, failure modes, abuse controls, and interaction with the existing lifecycle.
