# Institutional Operating Loop Concept

## Purpose

Record the proposed operating pattern for converting a BHG direction, question, problem, or idea into governed work, executing everything legitimately automatable, and stopping only at a mandatory human authority gate.

## Principle

Inputs must be interpreted in relation to the existing ecosystem, architecture, dependencies, evidence requirements, lifecycle state, and authority boundaries. Work must be executed as far as authorized; human approval, independent verification, and normative authority must never be simulated.

## Operating loop

```text
Direction / Question / Problem / Idea
        ↓
Interpret
        ↓
Map to ecosystem
        ↓
Identify location, dependencies and authority
        ↓
Classify maturity and governance state
        ↓
Design required work
        ↓
Implement authorized work
        ↓
Test / audit / generate evidence
        ↓
Prepare change for review
        ↓
MANDATORY HUMAN GATE
        ↓
Merge / approve / continue as authorized
        ↓
Post-merge verification
        ↓
Detect next gaps
        ↓
Continue loop
```

## Maturity

An idea may remain a concept without implementation. Possible states include concept, research, proposal, experiment, validated change, candidate standard, and institutional standard. Registration alone never constitutes adoption.

## Human boundary

A Draft document remains Draft until the applicable authority formally changes its status. No autonomous process may manufacture approval or normative authority.

## Intended benefits

Reduce founder dependency, preserve institutional traceability, accelerate routine execution through automation, and concentrate human attention on decisions that genuinely require human authority.

## Non-goals

This concept does not create governance authority, authorize autonomous approval, replace independent human review, establish succession rules, establish emergency powers, or declare this model canonical.

## Validation question

Future work should determine whether this loop improves execution time and continuity without weakening evidence, authority, traceability, or independent verification.
