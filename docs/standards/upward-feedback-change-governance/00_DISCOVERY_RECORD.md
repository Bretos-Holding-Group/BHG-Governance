# Discovery Record

## Purpose

Preserve the origin and current understanding of the BHG Upward Feedback & Change Governance concept without declaring it an institutional standard.

## Origin

The concept emerged during ZivaID R00 market-validation governance work when a documentary-status problem exposed a broader requirement: local operational discoveries should be able to become reusable ecosystem capabilities through evidence-based escalation.

A second requirement was identified: BHG continuity must not depend on the founder personally discovering every problem or approving every improvement. Designers, programmers, engineers and other operational contributors must have a durable upward-feedback path.

## Core principle

Any authorized contributor may identify a problem or propose an improvement. Proposal origin or hierarchy does not create authority. Institutional change requires evidence, review, appropriate authority and traceability.

## Succession objective

The model is intended to reduce key-person dependency by making organizational learning and improvement institutional processes rather than personal memory.

## Anti-abuse principle

Openness to feedback must not become unrestricted change authority. Proposals are progressively filtered, tested and exposed according to evidence, cost, risk and blast radius.

## Current decision

This record establishes discovery only. It does **not** authorize operational use, mandatory adoption, or BHG-wide standardization.

## Candidate lifecycle

F0 Observation → F1 Proposal → F2 Fundamented Proposal → F3 Experiment Candidate → F4 Closed Controlled Test → F5 Limited Real-World Deployment → F6 Local/Subsidiary Scale → F7 Validated Local Change → F8 BHG Standard Candidate → F9 Institutional Standard.

## Open status

The concept is a candidate institutional capability. F4–F9 require actual evidence and governance gates; they must not be fabricated by documentation alone.
