# Validation and Evidence Plan

## F0 — Observation
Evidence: dated observation record and originating context.
Current disposition: demonstrated conceptually by the ZivaID documentary-status discovery.

## F1 — Proposal
Evidence: explicit proposed solution and origin record.
Current disposition: documented in the discovery package.

## F2 — Fundamented Proposal
Evidence: problem statement, evidence, impact, reasoning, cost/risk/scope assessment.
Current disposition: candidate model has sufficient rationale for controlled design work; formal evidence package still required.

## F3 — Experiment Candidate
Evidence: hypothesis, success/failure criteria, metrics, experiment owner, rollback and resource authorization.
Current disposition: design-ready; not yet operationally authorized.

## F4 — Closed Controlled Test
Required evidence: reproducible controlled tests showing the lifecycle can correctly classify transitions, prevent unauthorized promotion, preserve failures and enforce gates.
Required artifact: controlled test report.

## F5 — Limited Real-World Deployment
Required evidence: pilot in one real local/subsidiary environment, bounded blast radius, monitoring and rollback.
Required artifact: pilot report.

## F6 — Local/Subsidiary Scale
Required evidence: stable operation at the intended local scope, incident analysis, adoption metrics and rollback readiness.
Required artifact: local scale validation report.

## F7 — Validated Local Change
Required evidence: reproducible positive outcome, limitations and owner acceptance.
Required artifact: local validation decision.

## F8 — BHG Standard Candidate
Required evidence: cross-entity applicability. Preferably demonstrated in at least two materially different contexts, or justified by a strong generalization analysis approved by the appropriate authority.
Required artifact: standard-candidacy assessment.

## F9 — Institutional Standard
Required evidence: formal adoption decision, normative specification, owner, effective version, compliance/verification mechanism, review cycle, revocation and supersession procedures.
Required artifact: institutional standard package.

## Current reality

Documentation can prepare and structure F4–F9, but cannot claim that those phases have occurred. Real-world validation must be performed with actual controlled evidence.
