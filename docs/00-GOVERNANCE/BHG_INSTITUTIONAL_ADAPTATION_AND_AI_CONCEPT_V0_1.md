# BHG Institutional Adaptation and AI Concept

## 1. Purpose

Record, without adoption, the concept of an institutional capability through which BHG can receive organizational change, feedback, questions and knowledge gaps; classify and prioritize them; acquire and validate missing information; and convert validated learning into governed institutional knowledge.

## 2. Core vision

An authorized BHG user, including the CEO, should be able to ask the institutional AI about the organization at any time. The system should answer from current, traceable and verifiable institutional evidence when sufficient knowledge exists. When it cannot answer, it should explicitly state the knowledge state and produce a traceable information-acquisition plan rather than inventing an answer.

## 3. Knowledge states

Conceptually, the future system should distinguish at least:

- KNOWN
- PARTIALLY_KNOWN
- UNKNOWN
- CONFLICTED
- STALE
- UNANSWERABLE_YET

Evidence, inference, hypothesis, recommendation and authorized decision must remain separate semantic classes.

## 4. Organizational adaptation

Every material change should have an institutional intake path, including legal/regulatory changes, technical changes, operational incidents, market changes, employee feedback, customer observations, audit findings and genuinely novel events.

The future capability should provide triage, priority, ownership, evidence requirements, response tracking, validation and institutional learning. It must reduce process latency rather than create procedural paralysis.

## 5. Feedback governance

Feedback should be accepted from all organizational levels and entities, including engineers, programmers, designers, operators and executives, while being subject to evidence, impact, priority, duplication, risk and experimental gates. Good ideas should be discoverable without allowing uncontrolled feedback volume to overwhelm governance capacity.

## 6. Continuity and succession concept

Institutional knowledge should not depend on the founder or any individual. Future qualified leaders, whether members of the Breto family or other organizational members, should be able to access governed institutional knowledge and understand requirements for roles they seek to occupy. Family continuity, ownership, governance authority and qualification are separate concepts.

## 7. Relationship to F0-F9

This concept does not modify the current F0-F9 lifecycle. Future validated changes should enter the existing lifecycle at the appropriate stage and return institutional learning to the normal governance cycle.

## 8. Relationship to Emergency Governance

Emergency Governance remains a separate future concept. An exceptional event may generate new evidence and learning, but no emergency mechanism is created or authorized by this document.

## 9. Non-goals

This concept does not:

- create an institutional AI;
- delegate governance authority to AI;
- create emergency authority;
- change succession rules;
- reserve offices for family members;
- change existing approval gates;
- authorize production automation;
- establish a canonical standard.

## 10. Future research questions

1. What evidence model must support trustworthy institutional answers?
2. How should conflicting evidence be resolved?
3. How should legal deadlines affect change prioritization?
4. Which decisions may be automated and which require human authority?
5. How should knowledge freshness be measured?
6. How should information-acquisition requests be routed and escalated?
7. How should feedback abuse be detected without suppressing legitimate dissent?
8. How should succession qualification be represented and audited?
9. What controls prevent AI from manufacturing authority or silently converting inference into policy?
10. How should the system prove that automation reduced rather than increased organizational latency?

## 11. Status

This document is a recorded concept only. It is not an adopted policy, standard, authority model or operational specification. Any implementation must be treated as a separate project and pass the applicable validation and approval lifecycle.
