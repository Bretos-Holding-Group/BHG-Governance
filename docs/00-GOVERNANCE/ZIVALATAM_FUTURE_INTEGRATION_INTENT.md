# ZivaLatam Future Integration Intent

## Purpose

This record documents BHG's strategic intent to evaluate ZivaLatam as a potential future first subsidiary of BHG.

It records future-oriented institutional intent only. It does not establish present ownership, control, subsidiary status, legal subordination, or governance authority over ZivaLatam.

## Current State

ZivaLatam is currently an independent operational entity/product with its own identity, systems, and operational rules.

Its systems are intentionally designed to remain compatible with BHG architectural and governance principles.

Current relationship:

**INDEPENDENT / FUTURE-INTEGRATION-CANDIDATE**

## Future Integration Principle

A future integration may occur only through a separate formal process that evaluates legal, organizational, governance, financial, technical, and operational implications and obtains the required approvals.

Future integration compatibility must not be interpreted as present institutional affiliation.

## Expected Future State

If formally approved and legally implemented, ZivaLatam may become the first BHG subsidiary, subject to the applicable corporate and legal instruments and to the BHG governance framework in force at that time.

The future integrated state is:

**INTEGRATED**

The transition is conditional and must never be inferred from this document alone.

## Bilateral Documentation Principle

BHG maintains this record from the BHG institutional perspective.

ZivaLatam should maintain a corresponding record from its own independent perspective. The existence of either record does not create the future relationship.

## Integration Gate

Before integration is recognized as effective, the applicable process must establish:

- current ownership and legal status;
- formal integration instrument(s);
- governance authority and boundaries;
- organizational and subsidiary relationship;
- technical and documentary dependencies;
- financial and operational implications;
- registry updates;
- post-integration verification.

## Non-Authority Clause

This document does not:

- create legal personality;
- establish ownership;
- establish a subsidiary relationship;
- impose present governance authority on ZivaLatam;
- replace a legal or corporate integration instrument;
- authorize operational changes in ZivaLatam.

It records BHG's future-oriented strategic intent and the conditions required before any integration can become effective.
