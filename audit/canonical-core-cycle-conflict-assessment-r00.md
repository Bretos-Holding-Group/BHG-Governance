## Cycle gate

Any discovery candidate participating in one of these cycles is HOLD until the complete path is semantically classified. The system may report, preserve, and trace the cycle but must not choose an edge to remove automatically.

## Conflict gate

A candidate touching a known normative conflict is HOLD for unconditional canonization. The candidate can remain in the discovery set and can be a provisional core candidate, but its authority claim must remain bounded until the conflict is resolved through the governing process.

## Interpretation

The assessment deliberately separates four states:

- structurally central;
- normatively authoritative;
- contractually foundational;
- unresolved/conflicted.

A high discovery score with unresolved authority is not a canonical promotion. It is a high-value review target.

## Current disposition

No cycle is automatically remediated. No normative conflict is automatically adjudicated. No Draft document is promoted. The next machine-verifiable stage is to populate each of the 60 rows with direct evidence from the four repositories and calculate a bounded qualification result.

## Acceptance criteria for Canonical Core R00

A document may enter the final Core set only if its inclusion rationale is reproducible, its ownership is supported, its authority claim is consistent with the applicable hierarchy, and any conflict/cycle is either resolved through evidence or explicitly represented as a bounded unresolved condition approved through the governance process.
